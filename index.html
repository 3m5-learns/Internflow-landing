The Problem

Internship programs are more fragmented than they need to be.

No unified view of intern status
Multiple tools needed to answer a single question.
Documents stored across different systems
No structured access control across disconnected locations.
Unclear lines of accountability
Hard to establish who approved what and when.
Manual attendance and reporting
Paper registers create gaps and inconsistencies.
Before
  • Applications via email or paper
  • Attendance in per-department registers
  • Documents in drives and physical files
  • No audit trail for approvals
  • HR and supervisors on different records
After
  • Structured intake with document uploads
  • Digital check-in with full attendance history
  • Role-gated documents in one platform
  • Full audit log of every action
  • Single system across all roles
Platform Capabilities

Everything the internship lifecycle requires, in one place.

InternFlow replaces disconnected processes with a single structured platform that covers the full internship cycle — from first application to completion certificate.

Application Management

Structured application intake with multi-step forms, required document uploads, and department-based routing into review or waiting queues.

Queue & Capacity Planning

Department-level candidate queues with capacity tracking, slot availability indicators, and recommended onboarding dates based on current intern load.

Department Administration

Department-scoped views for department administrators, including intern oversight, capacity settings, and department-level reporting.

Attendance Tracking

Digital check-in and check-out with structured records, attendance history, working-hour calculation, and downloadable attendance reports.

Daily Log Submission

Interns submit structured daily logs covering tasks, skills, and challenges. Supervisors review and verify logs within the same platform.

Performance Evaluations

Structured supervisor evaluations with scored criteria — discipline, technical skills, communication, work quality, and teamwork — and average scoring.

Document Management

File sharing within the platform, scoped by department or specific interns. Access controlled by role to protect sensitive documents.

Notifications

In-platform notifications from administrators to interns and supervisors. Targeted by department, specific users, or sent organization-wide.

Completion & Certification

Mark internships complete, generate branded completion certificates as PDF documents, and maintain full internship records for institutional reference.

Role-Based Access

The right access for every stakeholder.

InternFlow enforces structured role boundaries so each person in the process — administrator, department head, supervisor, or intern — sees and does exactly what their role requires.

Administrator

  • Review and approve applications
  • Manage all departments and interns
  • Send organization-wide notifications
  • Access audit logs and reporting
  • Generate completion certificates
  • Manage user accounts

Department Admin

  • Oversee department-scoped interns
  • Manage department capacity
  • Monitor attendance and logs
  • Send notifications to department interns
  • Access department-level reporting

Supervisor

  • View assigned interns
  • Monitor daily attendance
  • Review and verify daily logs
  • Conduct performance evaluations
  • Assign tasks to interns
  • Share documents with interns

Intern

  • Check in and check out daily
  • View personal attendance history
  • Submit daily logs
  • Access assigned tasks
  • View evaluations and documents
  • Download attendance reports

Security & Accountability

Built for institutional standards from day one.

Internship programs handle personal information, uploaded documents, and administrative decisions. InternFlow is built with controlled access and structured accountability throughout.

JWT Authentication

Secure token-based authentication with automatic refresh and session management across all roles.

Dual-Layer Authorization

Role-based access enforced at both the API and interface layers. Unauthorized requests are rejected at the server regardless of the frontend state.

Department-Level Data Isolation

Department administrators and supervisors are scoped to their own department's data. Cross-department access is blocked by default.

Controlled Document Access

Documents are accessible only to users with appropriate role permissions. Sharing is deliberate — not accidental.

Implemented at Nepal Rastra Bank.

InternFlow was implemented in an institutional environment at Nepal Rastra Bank, the central bank of Nepal. This provided a real-world setting in which the platform was refined around the operational requirements and workflows of a regulated financial institution.

The implementation informed how InternFlow handles department-level administration, role-based access boundaries, structured record-keeping, and the audit trail required in an institutional context.

Who It's For

Built for organizations managing structured internship programs.

InternFlow is designed for institutional settings with multiple departments, defined administrative roles, and a need for structured oversight and records.

Banks & Financial Institutions
